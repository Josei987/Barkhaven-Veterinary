Meet the BarkHaven team!

Care for all pets!

We are very dedicated to taking care of beloved pets. Our team is especially dedicated to providing the best care for every animal that comes through our doors. At BarkHaven Veterinary, we provide thoughtful, dependable care for pets at every stage of life. From wellness exams and vaccinations to nutritional guidance and treatment when they are unwell, our team takes the time to understand each animal's needs. We welcome dogs, cats, and other beloved companions with patience and kindness, while partnering with their families to help them live healthier, happier lives.

Origins

It all started from 2018 when BarkHaven Veterinary began. Dr. Kaya Bennett noticed that many families in our neighborhood were traveling across town for compassionate veterinary care. With a small clinic, a big-hearted team, and her rescue dog, Clover, by her side, she opened BarkHaven as a welcoming place where every pet could feel safe. What started with one exam room has grown into a trusted community practice, but our promise remains the same: thoughtful care for every animal and the people who love them.

Join our team!

Our team brings together compassionate veterinarians, skilled veterinary nurses, and friendly client-care specialists who believe every pet deserves patience, respect, and excellent care. We support one another, keep learning, and celebrate the small victories that make a difference in an animal's life. At BarkHaven, we are always glad to meet people who share our love of animals and our commitment to their families.
